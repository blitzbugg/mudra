## Third-Party Assets

Some assets may be adapted from third-party works.

Such assets must include their original source, creator, and applicable license in `metadata.json`.

If an asset is subject to a license other than CC BY 4.0, that license takes precedence for that specific asset.

Contributors must not submit third-party artwork unless they have verified that the work can legally be redistributed and modified under its applicable license.

## Cultural Knowledge and Copyright

The cultural subject represented by an asset is not necessarily owned by Mudra or its contributors.

Mudra does not claim ownership over the cultural traditions, names, practices, symbols, or historical knowledge represented by its assets.

The license applies to the **specific creative artwork contributed to Mudra**, not to the underlying cultural tradition itself.

## Attribution

When attribution is required, users should follow the attribution information provided in the asset's metadata.

A recommended attribution format is:

> [Asset Name] by [Contributor], via Mudra, licensed under CC BY 4.0.

## Questions

If you are unsure whether an asset can be contributed to Mudra, open an issue or discussion before submitting it.
